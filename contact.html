Contact

JSON Keyper is a one-person project, so messages sent here come straight to me, Ashish Singh. I read all of them, and replies can take a few days.

Things I would particularly like to hear about:

  • A JSON document the tool handles wrongly. Include a small sample that shows the problem (remove anything private first), the output format you chose, and what you expected instead.
  • Mistakes in a guide. Tell me which article and what is wrong - an outdated command, a claim that does not hold on your system, a broken example.
  • Formats or features you are missing. Most of the tool's output formats exist because someone needed them.

If you are comfortable on GitHub, bug reports can also go straight to the project's issue tracker, where you can follow the fix. Or email me directly at admin@jsonkeyper.com.

Please do not send API keys, tokens, or personal data in a sample. I do not need them to reproduce a bug.
