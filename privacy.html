Privacy Policy

Welcome to JSON Keyper. Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. We are committed to maintaining transparency regarding how your information is handled.

No Data Storage

JSON Keyper does not store any user data. Any JSON you paste on our website is processed entirely in your own browser. This means that we do not collect, store, or share your data. Your privacy is completely protected while using our tool.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics such as page views and session duration. This helps us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Google Analytics does not identify individual users. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Google AdSense may use cookies to provide relevant ads based on your visits to this and other websites. However, JSON Keyper itself does not collect any personally identifiable information.

Cookies

JSON Keyper does not use any first-party cookies for tracking purposes. However, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads and collect analytics data based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these other sites. Please review their privacy policies before providing them with your personal information.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed of any changes. This policy was last updated on June 23, 2026.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our privacy practices, please reach out via our contact page or email us at admin@jsonkeyper.com.
